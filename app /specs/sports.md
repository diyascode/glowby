## 1. Scope & tie-breakers (AUTHORITATIVE)
Owns: results, scores, stats, standings, records, transactions, injuries/availability, competition facts. Law handoff (Branch 4): doping, match-fixing, financial fair play, abuse allegations — Law's source rules and harm gate; a guilt claim is never scoreable here. Business handoff: franchise valuations, sponsorship economics, stadium financing. Entertainment boundary: esports COMPETITION results score here (tournament organizers are the record-keepers); the games as creative works stay in Entertainment. Politics handoff: stadium-funding fights, boycotts, hosting controversies. Straddle handshake: score this component, mark PROVISIONAL, attach external_straddle_risk; straddle rule = LOWER of the two. Principles: *a person can corroborate, but only the official record can settle.* STATE OVER TIME: at what time, under which official record, at which state of confirmation. Hack-proof backstop: the insider flag caps at provisional — a hacked or parody account can at most reach "credibly reported"; only official confirmation lifts the cap.

## 2. Scoring procedure (in order)
1. Entity resolution: WHICH player (common names, transliterations, single-name Brazilian players, junior/senior), club (namesakes), competition, season; a stat claim without competition scope is unresolved.
2. Route to four branches; facts MOVE between branches as they age (transfer: Branch 2 → Branch 1 on club confirmation).
3. Record lookups first (Branch 1): governing body, official club/league channel, or official data provider; match settles high, contradict → low, flagged. Definition-pin before lookup: competitions, era, per-game vs total, regular season vs playoffs. STAT-PROVIDER CONFLICT RULE: record-keepers splitting on DEFINITIONS → DEPENDS ON DEFINITION with each provider's definition and value; never average, never louder-source-wins.
4. Transaction ladder (Branch 2): agreed terms ≠ medical ≠ signed ≠ officially announced — score the exact rung. Verified-insider flag applies here ONLY: lifts to credibly-reported, never settled.
5. Temporal state: REPORTED → × PROVISIONAL_CAP 0.85; OFFICIALLY CONFIRMED → cap lifted, settle-eligible.
6. Interested-party check (Branch 3): contested self-serving claims (transfer denials, disciplinary disputes) → × CONFLICT_MULT 0.30 even via an official channel — the channel is a party to the contest.
7. Echo collapse (85%, semantic copies): fifty outlets repeating one insider = ONE source. Corroboration +0.6 per genuinely independent source beyond the first, cap +1.8.
8. Correction workflow: record_version = current, corrected, vacated, under-appeal, superseded. A claim matching the record at the time reads "accurate at checked_at, later revised" — never retroactively false. Vacated → DUAL DISPLAY (on-field result plus official record status).
9. Formula: Score = MIN(9.9, (Base + Corroboration) × Temporal × InterestedParty). Compound = MIN across sub-claims. The timestamp is load-bearing.

## 3. Evidence hierarchy
Tier 1 (9.2): governing bodies/official channels — FIFA, UEFA, IOC, NBA/NFL/MLB/NHL, national FAs, ICC/cricket boards, World Athletics; confirmed club announcements; Opta/Stats Perform; routed by competition → regional_source_route (carries league conventions). Tier 2 (7.0): ESPN, BBC Sport, The Athletic, Sky Sports and peers via MBFC + NewsGuard. Tier 3 (5.8): verified-insider flag (Romano/Wojnarowski class) — corroborate only, never settle. Tier 4 (3.5): unrated, aggregators, content farms. Finding aids: Transfermarkt (fees/valuations are ESTIMATES), Wikipedia — never anchor. Coverage-gap rule: one official source SETTLES routine facts in low-coverage competitions (women's, lower leagues, youth, regional); coverage_gap_reason recorded; "not independently covered" ≠ "not verified". Esports fallback: organizer channel/API → vetted archival registries (Liquipedia-class), routine results only → unverified; contested rulings require the organizer/successor body; a team tweet confirms participation, never results.

## 4. Verified-insider membership rule
Requires BOTH verified identity (no parody/unverified handles) AND confirmed track record. 🟡 INSIDER_MIN_HITS (proposed, needs sign-off): ≥5 definitive reports confirmed official in trailing 24 months, per sport (status doesn't transfer), ≥80% confirmed. Cold-streak rule, velocity-adjusted per sport_template: fast markets (soccer) → 2-consecutive-miss cliff; low-velocity sports → rolling floor (suspend below 75% accuracy, trailing 6 months). Only DEFINITIVE reports count either way; hedges score nothing, cost nothing. Time decay weights recent reports. HEDGE FLAG: "monitoring", "interested in", "talks expected" hard-capped at HEDGE_CAP 4.5 even from flagged insiders — just above the fleet prediction cap 4.0.

## 5. Special protocols / harm gates
- Live-state machine: pre-game, live, halftime/intermission, full-time, official-final, corrected-final, postponed, abandoned, replay-under-review. Live caps: official live feed ≤ 8.5, non-official ≤ 7.0, 9.9 only at official-final.
- Betting-integrity ENFORCEMENT GATE: betting_integrity_risk high + unverified/unflagged source → automatic high-priority escalation AND INTEGRITY_CEILING 3.0. Anonymous injury rumors: higher bar, never presented as likely. Sportsbooks/fantasy platforms settle their OWN lines/scoring only, never athlete facts.
- Privacy (do-not-amplify): official status designation scoreable; medical diagnosis, mental health, pregnancy, addiction, family matters — never speculate. Team availability claims are official-but-interested.
- Officiating/rules interpretation: fact only after officials/governing body rule; else not scoreable. "Greatest of all time" → NOT SCOREABLE (the Society rule).
- Fake screenshots: verify against the insider's ACTUAL account; fabricated → media handoff + fake-attribution. Clips verify against the official game log; edited/miscontextualized → media handoff. Lineups only via official channels/broadcast data on the official feed.
- "Record" marketing: pin competition, era, gender category, age cutoff, regular-season/playoff, friendly/exhibition scope. Retired records → superseded treatment. Suspensions: appeal_status + checked_at. College: commitment ≠ NLI ≠ enrollment ≠ eligibility ≠ roster. Translation/transliteration checks.
- Mandatory automated recheck sweeps at checked_at +12h and +24h for claims scored during/within 2h of a live match.
