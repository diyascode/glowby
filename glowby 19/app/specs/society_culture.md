# Society & Culture Judge — Distilled Config (Spec v1.3, 2026-07-05)

> Conversion: spec 0–99 → Glowby 0.0–9.9 (÷10, one decimal): tier bases 8.5/8.0/7.0/5.0/2.5; corroboration +0.5/cap +1.2; SINGLE_STUDY_CAP 5.5; CAUSAL_OVERREACH_CAP 6.0. Multipliers unchanged. Compute in decimals, round once.

## 1. Scope & tie-breakers (AUTHORITATIVE)
Owns: social trends, religion, traditions, identity, demographics, lifestyle, community practices, human-interest generalizations. Politics handoff: the law fight is Politics; a movement's size and character stays here. Law handoff: crimes by/against groups — cases are Law; group-level statistics stay here (with the group-generalization guard). Health handoff: diet/wellness lifestyle stays UNLESS it makes a medical claim — then Health. Science handoff: "studies show" with a hard-science core routes to Science; social-science stays with the shakier-evidence treatment. History & Geography handoff: heritage/cultural-origin claims (often nationalistic) dual-tag with History. Straddle handshake: score this component, mark PROVISIONAL, attach external_straddle_risk; heavy straddle traffic with Politics/Law/Conflict; straddle rule = LOWER of two. Principle: *a sample can be loud without being large — score what it stands for, not how far it traveled.* NOT SCOREABLE is a SUCCESSFUL outcome.

## 2. Scoring procedure (in order)
1. Normalize, split, preserve original wording.
2. Route by type; record gates: route_status, measurable_core_status, definition_pinned, source_origin_found, representativeness_result. Mandatory 4b escape hatch, explicit stop reasons: no metric, vague population, opinion-only, satire/fiction, personal experience, insufficient evidence. Don't force a score.
3. Group-generalization guard (runs early; the harm gate): negative behavioral generalizations about ethnic/religious/identity groups demand Tier-1 statistical support, exact group and behavior definitions, the denominator, BASE RATE plus a relevant COMPARATOR group, non-stigmatizing wording. Essentialist claims ("group X is [trait]") → NOT SCOREABLE. sensitive_group_flag → human review before public display.
4. Definition-pin the metric for every number/trend claim (divorce rate crude vs refined; affiliation identity vs attendance vs belief; loneliness by which instrument).
5. Chase the number to its origin: score reflects the primary statistical source traced to, not the outlet. Survey-quality check: probability sample vs opt-in panel vs self-selected viral poll — the method decides the tier.
6. Echo collapse (85%, semantic copies). Corroboration +0.5 per genuinely independent originating source beyond the first, cap +1.2.
7. Representativeness check on every generalization: Pass ×1.00, Partial ×0.70, Fail ×0.40.
8. Evidence-conflict check: aligned, mixed, contradictory, incomparable_definitions. Contradictory → interpretive-contested or unverified — NEVER highest-source-wins. Incomparable definitions = definition mismatch (different penalty; verdict explains the sources measure different things).
9. Type flags: Type 2 single-study → hard cap until a review/meta-analysis exists. CAUSAL OVERREACH: a causal verb ("because of", "leads to", "creates") on correlational data → CAUSAL_OVERREACH_CAP 6.0 regardless of sample quality; a SINGLE correlational study asserting causation takes MIN(5.5, 6.0) = 5.5. Type 3 conditional insider treatment. Type 4a one-sided sourcing → LEAN_ONESIDED_MULT ×0.75 (lean from MBFC; ON only in 4a).
10. Formula: Published = ROUND(MIN((Base + Corroboration) × Represent × ConflictMult × LeanMult, Cap)); Cap = single-study cap for one-study Type 2, else 9.9. Compound = MIN across scoreable sub-claims; NOT SCOREABLE displayed alongside, never averaged. Green minimum: ≥1 high-quality origin AND representativeness Pass — a high base alone never suffices.

## 3. Claim types
1 Number: Tier-1 primary stats; trace back; pin first; lean OFF. 2 "Studies show": Tier-2 peer review; single-study cap; causal-gap ladder ("linked to" ≠ "because of"); lean OFF. 3 Culture: Tier-2 ethnography + the group's own representatives, CONDITIONAL insider treatment; subtypes 3+: doctrine (insider-authoritative ×0.95 descriptive), practice-prevalence (needs population data — a doctrine source cannot carry a prevalence number), representation (single-perspective check), origin (dual-tag History); lean OFF. 5 Anecdote-to-trend: trend-panic default — representativeness FAIL until population-level data; platform data verifies the platform-level claim only; lean OFF. 4a Interpretive contested-empirical (measurable core): scoreable; one-sided sourcing ×0.75; lean ON. 4b Interpretive normative (value judgment): NOT SCOREABLE, neutral, never a red "false". 4a/4b rule: no nameable measurable core → 4b. Measurable-core tree: (1) metric nameable? (2) denominator/population? (3) time window? (4) geography? (5) evidence type identifiable? No on (1) → 4b; no on (2)–(5) → the pin supplies it, else measurable_core_status ambiguous → conservative or stopped.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy
T1 (8.5): primary stats — US Census Bureau, Pew, GSS/NORC, Gallup, BLS, national statistical offices, World Values Survey, Eurobarometer, Pew Global Attitudes. T2 (8.0): peer-reviewed sociology/anthropology/demography (Crossref/DOAJ checks), field studies, university centers. T3 (7.0): quality press tracing to T1/T2 — scored by the source traced to. T4 (5.0): opinion/advocacy, lean-coded (Heritage, AEI, Brookings, CAP) — 4a only. T5 (2.5): social/anecdotal/viral — almost never carries a generalization alone. Global fallback ladder: national statistics office → census/survey agency → cross-national survey → peer-reviewed regional study → quality press tracing to data (regional_source_route; expands the search, never lowers the bar). Local expertise (Indigenous, minority, diaspora, regional) is CONTEXT, not proof. Press quality ≠ evidence quality. Freshness: demographic ~1 year, doctrine long-lived, viral trends days.

## 5. Named traps / protocols
Representativeness stretch (signature trap) incl. the moral-panic/tide-pod pattern (default FAIL); off-ramp (Control constants): probability-sample survey of the targeted population N ≥ 1,000, OR registry data deviating from the baseline year across ≥3 distinct geographic areas. Conditional insider treatment: descriptive own-practice ×0.95 (subject-accountable); self-serving/contested → CONFLICT_MULT ×0.30; doctrine wants BOTH scholarship and insider confirmation, either alone → single-perspective flag. Opinions in fact costumes → 4b. Survey laundering — TEN-item checklist: probability sampling, panel recruitment, sample size, weighting, question wording, sponsor, field dates, mode effects, (9) SYNTHETIC/BOT FILTRATION, (10) WEIGHTING VARIANCE (extreme factors); failing 9 or 10 drops the instrument to Tier 4/5 automatically. Translation trap (original_language + translated_claim_text stored; translation-risk flag). Platform-amplification trap. Definition drift. Cherry-picked social windows (full-series trend, window-shift test, per-capita normalization). Single-study syndrome (replication problem).
