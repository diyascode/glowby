# Entertainment & Media Judge — Distilled Config (Spec v1.2, 2026-07-05)

> Conversion: spec 0–99 → Glowby 0.0–9.9 (÷10, one decimal): record-keeper 9.5, trade 8.2, bare-fact 8.0, personal-claim outlet 6.0, LEAK_CAP 7.0, digital-engagement cap 7.5, ambiguity cap 5.0, corroboration +0.6/cap +1.8. Multipliers unchanged. Compute in decimals, round once.

## 1. Scope & tie-breakers (AUTHORITATIVE)
Owns: the creative output and the people — who made what, release dates, casting, official-metric performance, awards, signings, public statements/acts of public figures. Business/Economy handoff: earnings, subscriber counts, stock moves; a purely money-defined "flop" straddles, lower-of-two. Law handoff: guilt never scoreable here; only the procedural shell ("charges were filed") stays, via Law's machinery incl. its harm gate for claims naming a person. Society & Culture handoff: "what this show says about us" is interpretation, not an entertainment fact. Technology handoff: platform tech claims (codec, resolution, AI tools). Straddle handshake: score this component, mark PROVISIONAL, attach external_straddle_risk; straddle rule = LOWER of two. Hybrid: hard-data spine (record-keepers) + gossip layer, often UNSCOREABLE (the most-used special verdict). Principles: *the publicist can confirm, but only the record can settle — and the gossip never settles. Verify public entertainment facts aggressively, discount promotional framing, refuse to score private speculation.*

## 2. Scoring procedure (in order)
1. Resolve the WORK and PERSON: remakes, re-releases, re-recordings, regional titles, game editions, same-name performers. REGISTRY ANCHORS mandatory: EIDR/ISAN (film/TV), ISRC (music); no ID → title+year+version fallback. Work ambiguity affecting the verdict → cap 5.0 or entity-ambiguous; PERSON ambiguity in a NEGATIVE claim inherits Law's identity handling.
2. Route to one of six lanes; one story routinely splits across lanes.
3. Record lookups first: dates, credits, grosses, chart positions, awards against that metric's record-keeper; match → high, contradict → low, flagged. Definition-pin first: WHICH chart, territory, week; nominal vs inflation-adjusted; nominated vs won.
4. Gossip sub-router (lane 4): SETTLED (officially confirmed by the person, rep, or a document) scores normally. PROVISIONAL (hard-news break from an outlet with an accountability track record on THAT claim type) → × PROVISIONAL_CAP 0.85 WITH EXPIRY: hold through the recheck window (casting/deal rumors 7–14 days), then STEP-DROP — day 15 unconfirmed flips to unverified + provisional-expired flag (a cliff, not a slope; death claims exempt). UNSCOREABLE (no official basis — anonymous "sources close to", speculation, body-language "experts") → no truth score: "personal claim, no reliable basis". Classify by claim basis, NOT outlet reputation.
5. Interested-party split (lane 3), THREE tiers: (a) own bare fact (release date, tracklist, casting) near-full; (b) soft hype → × PROMO_MULT 0.30; (c) comparative superlative requires record-keeper confirmation, else definition-gated/unverified — never scored on the penalty alone.
6. Leak handling: authentic-but-unofficial caps at LEAK_CAP 7.0 unless verified by a named accountable trade or public record; embargoed/leaked/hacked-account material flagged; artifact authenticity → media specialist.
7. Quote verification (lane 5): primary recording or transcript, never paraphrase; pull-quote check against the FULL review; quote-context ladder: exact, fair-paraphrase, clipped-but-fair, misleading-clip, reversed-meaning, fabricated, synthetic-suspected (→ media handoff).
8. Correction tracking: score against the CORRECTED version + correction-applied (the Business restated-financials rule).
9. Echo collapse (85%, semantic copies): one exclusive rewritten by fifty aggregators = ONE source; one press kit = ONE source. Corroboration +0.6 per genuinely independent source, cap +1.8.
10. Caps in order: PROMO penalty → provisional cap → streaming cap (≤7.5) → ceiling 9.9. Compound = MIN across scoreable sub-claims; UNSCOREABLE displayed alongside, never averaged.

Lanes: 1 official-record fact; 2 industry/business fact (greenlights, deals, casting, budgets — trades tier; outlets syndicating a trade = ONE evidence item); 3 interested-party; 4 GOSSIP; 5 quote/attribution; 6 straddle/handoff (guilt keeps only the shell).

## 3. Evidence hierarchy (record-keepers per metric)
Bases: record-keeper 9.5, trade 8.2, interested-party bare fact 8.0, personal-claim outlet 6.0. Record-keepers PER METRIC: Comscore, Box Office Mojo, The Numbers (box office); Billboard, Official Charts Co., Oricon, GfK, Luminate (charts — route by territory → regional_source_route); awarding bodies' own results (nominated ≠ won pinned); AFI Catalog, studio-confirmed credits, guild databases (IMDb finding aid only); RT/Metacritic for their OWN metric only (critic vs audience never interchangeable); primary video/transcript/verified post (quotes); IFPI, platform charts (platform-specific, never "the charts"). Games: publisher/store pages (release facts), ESRB/PEGI (ratings), Metacritic/OpenCritic (own metric only), SteamDB (cautiously), GamesIndustry.biz (trade). Trades: Variety, THR, Deadline. Conditional (tier per claim type, not brand): TMZ — STRONG on documented hard breaks (deaths, arrests, legal documents), WEAK on speculation; People, Us Weekly — PR-confirmed only. Low: Daily Mail, The Sun, National Enquirer, gossip blogs (default toward unscoreable). DIGITAL ENGAGEMENT CAP 7.5 (generalized STREAM_CAP): self-reported streaming AND social metrics; anonymous platform leaks and non-audited "record-breaking" cap lower; the verdict always names the metric and its self-reported status.

## 4. Named traps / protocols
Promotion wearing a press badge (signature trap: all roads to one studio leak = ONE interested source). Death hoax (highest-risk item): SETTLED only via family/representative, employer/label, or official record; wire obituary = provisional-strong; anything else UNSCOREABLE + death-hoax-pattern flag; verdict never repeats the claim as likely; 100% eval gate. Box-office spin: definition-pin (nominal/adjusted, domestic/worldwide, 3-day vs 5-day); superlatives take the History-style gate — split → DEPENDS ON DEFINITION. Streaming-metric mush. Pull-quote laundering. Fake announcements and fan-made media: counts only on studio/label own channels or an accountable trade with a named reporter, else fake-announcement flag; synthetic → media handoff with halt rule. Metric substitution: verify the EXACT metric named, never convert → definition-mismatch; GAMING CLAUSE: registered players ≠ units sold ≠ concurrent players ≠ MAU — interested-party "best-seller" from sign-ups auto-fires definition-mismatch + the comparative-superlative rule. Awards precision: nominated ≠ shortlisted ≠ won; actor vs film; category matters. Privacy line: health rumors, outing, pregnancy speculation, claims about MINOR CHILDREN → UNSCOREABLE by default + do-not-amplify; legal-allegation gossip keeps only the procedural shell via Law's harm gate.
