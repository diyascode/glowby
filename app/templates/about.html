Why Glowby exists

Short-form video is how our generation gets news — and it's the least fact-checked medium on the internet. Professional fact-checkers write articles; nobody reads a 1,200-word article about a 40-second TikTok. Glowby closes that gap: paste the video, get one honest score and every source, in under a minute.

What we believe

Receipts over trust-me. Glowby never asks you to believe it — every verdict shows the sources it's built on, so you can check its work. An unverifiable verdict is a worthless one.

Honest about uncertainty. When the evidence isn't there, Glowby says so and shows no score at all. Uncertainty is never dressed up as a number, and a perfect 10.0 is never awarded — no claim is beyond all future evidence.

Mistakes get fixed, not buried. Every result has a report button, anyone can force a re-check, and corrections are tracked. Read the full corrections policy.

Who's behind it

Glowby was founded and built by Diya Sekhon, a 17-year-old who got tired of watching false claims travel faster than their corrections. It was designed, built, and launched in one summer using AI-assisted development — a solo founder directing every scoring rule, fairness policy, and honesty principle in the system. Glowby is independent: no ads, no sponsors, and nobody can pay to change a score.
