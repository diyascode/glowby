What should Glowby check?

Before Glowby checks anything

To fact-check, Glowby sends what you submit — the link you paste, the text you type, or the photo you choose — to AI services that analyze it:

  • Anthropic — reads the transcript, video frames and evidence to judge claims
  • OpenAI — turns speech in videos into text
  • Hive — checks whether photos or video frames are AI-generated (only when that check runs)
  • Google — captions, the fact-check database, and reverse image search

Nothing else is sent: no account, no contacts, no location, no tracking profile. Photos are analyzed and discarded, never stored. Each service protects the data under its own policy, linked in our privacy policy.

You can review this any time on the privacy page.
