# History & Geography Judge — Distilled Config (Spec v1.3, 2026-07-05)

> Conversion: spec 0–99 → Glowby 0.0–9.9 (÷10, one decimal): ConsensusCap 2.5; SINGLE_CAUSE_CAP 6.0; attestation ladder 9.9/8.5/6.5/4.0; bonuses +0.5/cap +2.0. Multipliers unchanged. Compute in decimals, round once.

## 1. Scope & tie-breakers (AUTHORITATIVE)
Owns: dates, historical events, capitals, borders, locations, place names, attributions/quotes, firsts and superlatives, discoveries about the past. Subject test: the past or physical world must BE the thing claimed, not the backdrop (school shooting → Law; school measles outbreak → Health). Politics handoff — RESOLVED tie-breaker: settled location → Geography; ACTIVE territorial/naming dispute → dual-tag with Politics, lower-of-two, neutral naming. Science handoff: the dating METHOD (radiocarbon, dendrochronology); the historical conclusion stays here. Conflict handoff: historical wars here, active ones Conflict (boundary: whether the shooting stopped). Society & Culture handoff: cultural-origin/heritage claims dual-tag. Straddle handshake: score this component, mark PROVISIONAL, attach external_straddle_risk; straddle rule = LOWER of two. Settled facts expire (Pluto, Everest) — **settled is EARNED against current consensus, never assumed**; when unsure, treat as novel. Special verdicts: DEPENDS ON DEFINITION, NOT SCOREABLE — headline_score null, band neutral; the frontend must never render null as zero.

## 2. Scoring procedure (in order)
1. PLACE-TIME resolution: the place AS IT WAS in the period claimed (historical name, modern equivalent, political unit at the time, calendar system, local Gregorian adoption, Old Style/New Style); pre-1582 dates pinned to a calendar. NON-WESTERN NORMALIZATION: Hijri, Hebrew, Chinese lunisolar, Buddhist, Saka, nengō, regnal years converted via concordance tables to proleptic Gregorian, verified normalized, displayed in both; unconvertible → date-ambiguous flag, conservative, never crashed.
2. Route to one of five types; the authoritative source flips by type.
3. Settled/novel switch: SETTLED → reference-match path (one good CURRENT anchor-tier match scores high); NOVEL/contested/unknown → full machine (unknown = novel). Earned-settled guard: CURRENT EXPERT consensus ≠ popular consensus; revision-prone domains (country names, borders, planetary classifications, measured heights, population records, recent discoveries) require a MANDATORY live current-authority check before the settled path; revised claims drop to the full machine, scored against the CURRENT answer + superseded-consensus flag.
4. Superlative definition-pin gate (type 4, before any counting): read the measuring rule from the rulebook — agree → score; split → DEPENDS ON DEFINITION with each standard's answer; not in rulebook → search the authority tier, no agreement → DEPENDS ON DEFINITION. Never count sources for a vague superlative.
5. Attribution tracing (type 3, NOT source-counting): EARLIEST ATTESTATION up the ladder primary document/recording → contemporaneous account → later biography → aggregators (finding aids only); a fabricated origin ZEROES the claim regardless of echo volume.
6. Echo collapse (85%, semantic copies) + citogenesis check, TIER-AGNOSTIC: any circular chain at any tier (Wikipedia→journalist→citation, or listicle-tier loops) = ZERO independent sources + circular-sourcing flag; loop detection scans the lower tier explicitly.
7. Score: base = best applicable source's tier score conditional on claim type, +0.5 per independent corroborator, capped at +2.0.
8. Caps in order: AlignmentMult (nationally/ideologically framed INTERPRETATION claims only, ×0.50) → ConsensusCap (contradicts current Tier-1 consensus → 2.5) → provenance zeroing (fabricated origin → 0.0) → definition gate → ceiling 9.9.
9. Compound = MIN across scoreable sub-claims; special verdicts displayed alongside, never averaged.

## 3. Claim types
1 Established fact: settled → reference-match, novel → full machine. 2 Interpretation & causation: full machine + national-framing check; single-cause claims about complex events take SINGLE_CAUSE_CAP 6.0 unless framed "one factor among several" (aligned with Society's causal-overreach cap). 3 Attribution & quotation: earliest-attestation tracing; provenance zeroing. 4 Superlative & record: definition-pin gate BEFORE scoring. 5 Novel/revisionist: always the full machine; single-paper discoveries inherit the fleet single-study caution.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy & authority matrix
Anchor (8.0–9.5): peer-reviewed history journals, university-press monographs, authenticated primary documents/archives (national archives, Library of Congress), USGS, NOAA, IHO, national mapping agencies, UNGEGN, edited reference works (Britannica, scholarly atlases). Middle (5.5–7.5): museums (Smithsonian), quality explainers (National Geographic), sourced journalism/documentary — SETTLED facts and corroboration only; barred from anchoring novel or interpretation claims. Lower/conditional (1.0–3.5): popular history without citations, TED/TEDx, "TIL" pop-facts — rarely anchors. Finding aids: Wikipedia, Wikiquote, Quote Investigator — never anchor; rate their citations. Matrix (anchor vs corroborate): scholarship anchors all five types; primary documents anchor Fact/Attribution/Novel, corroborate Interpretation/Superlative; mapping/standards agencies anchor Fact+Superlative (rulebook), corroborate Novel; edited reference works anchor settled Fact only, NEVER a novel finding; museums/explainers corroborate only. Dual-role rule: a national archive is authoritative for its DOCUMENTS, CONFLICTED on its NATION'S NARRATIVE. Home-region routing → regional_source_route.

## 5. Named traps / protocols
Expired settled fact (signature trap): handled by the earned-settled guard (step 3). Viral misquote: attestation-ladder caps 9.9 primary doc, 8.5 contemporaneous, 6.5 later biography, 4.0 aggregator-only; WORDING ladder: exact primary (full); verified paraphrase (capped — "meaning supported; wording not exact"); translated quote (translation flag; transliterated names same); attributed-later (biography-rung cap); documented misquote (zeroed); no attestation (unverified). Vague superlative — rulebook seeds: tallest mountain (Everest, agree); longest river (Nile/Amazon, split); largest desert, oldest city, oldest university, largest empire (split); largest country, longest border (agree w/ caveat); deepest lake (Baikal, agree); oldest democracy / first civilization (usually DEPENDS ON DEFINITION or NOT SCOREABLE). First-claim protocol: pin the sense (first documented, widely accepted, in a country, surviving evidence, modern version, disputed tradition); unpinned "first" takes the gate. Historical-superlative sub-template pins EVIDENCE BASIS, SCOPE, THRESHOLD before the gate; unpinnable → NOT SCOREABLE. Nationalist framing: ×0.50 applied symmetrically; archives verify documents, not narratives. Map-projection trap: convert map-appearance claims into the underlying area/distance/projection claim; verify against authority figures, never visual maps. Disputed-name trap: UNGEGN/ISO + dual-name display; verify the fact under either name; the naming FIGHT routes to Politics; locked template: "The location is administered by X and claimed by Y; the naming/sovereignty dispute is outside this category and is dual-tagged to Politics." Fabricated historical media: the media agent authenticates the artifact, this agent verifies the textual claim; an image is never evidence before the handoff returns. HALT RULE: definitive FABRICATED verdict halts processing → unverified + fabricated-media-handoff (or provenance-zeroed when the artifact IS the claimed origin).

## 6. Bands, states, output
Bands: 8.0–9.9 green, 5.0–7.9 orange, below 5.0 red; special verdicts neutral, null score; ceiling 9.9 (10.0 never awarded). verdict_state: settled-verified, novel-corroborated, depends-on-definition, attribution-verified, attribution-zeroed, superseded-consensus, consensus-capped, not-scoreable, unverified. reason_code: current-consensus-match, consensus-contradiction, definition-split, weak-attestation, circular-sourcing, national-framing, single-cause-capped. verdict_sentence: DEPENDS ON DEFINITION lists each standard's answer; "couldn't verify" never "false" except consensus/record contradiction; no intent language; VERDICT-LEAD RULE (verified core first). Fields: headline_score = MIN across scoreable sub-claims (null for special verdicts); verdict_state; band; reason_code; verdict_sentence; provisional; place_time; attribution_chain (earliest attestation, rung, path); definition_panel (each standard, authority, answer); regional_source_route; flags[] (superseded-consensus, consensus-capped, provenance-zeroed, circular-sourcing, depends-on-definition, national-framing, projection-claim, disputed-name, fabricated-media-handoff, echo-collapsed, ratings-stale, external_straddle_risk); audit_trace_id (sources accepted AND rejected, attestation, definition rule, resolver result, caps, exact public wording); checked_at.
